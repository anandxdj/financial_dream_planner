### 3.7 Reverse Proxy Cookie & Streaming Boundary
- **Cookie Architecture**:
  - In production (`NODE_ENV=production`), cookies use the RFC 6265bis secure `__Host-` prefix:
    - `__Host-access_token`: `httpOnly: true`, `secure: true`, `sameSite: lax`, `path: "/"`.
    - `__Host-refresh_token`: `httpOnly: true`, `secure: true`, `sameSite: lax`, `path: "/"`.
    - `__Host-csrf_token`: `httpOnly: false`, `secure: true`, `sameSite: lax`, `path: "/"`.
  - `frontend/src/proxy.ts` correctly detects `__Host-refresh_token` or `refresh_token` and gates unauthenticated requests to `/dashboard*` and `/onboarding*`.
  - Frontend rewrites in `frontend/next.config.ts` rewrite `/api/v1/:path*` to `${API_ORIGIN}/api/v1/:path*`, allowing browser traffic to remain same-origin and preserving cookie semantics and CSRF validation.
- **Streaming Boundary (SSE)**:
  - Backend `/api/v1/runs/:id/events` explicitly sets:
    - `Content-Type: text/event-stream`
    - `Cache-Control: no-cache, no-transform`
    - `Connection: keep-alive`
    - `X-Accel-Buffering: no`
    - Immediate `res.flushHeaders()` call and 15s heartbeat interval.
  - Standalone Next.js reverse proxy handles chunked responses without buffer interruption when `no-transform` is present.
