# AI and Research

LangGraph orchestrates financial-state, planning, research, risk, and critic nodes only after the deterministic engine exists. Provider adapters normalize a Chat-Completions-compatible primary endpoint and Gemini fallback. A run never switches providers after user-visible tokens begin.

Tools are tenant-scoped reads, deterministic calculators, scenario-draft creation, and approved research. Models cannot execute SQL/HTTP/filesystem operations, apply scenarios, accept drift, or change baselines.

Tavily discovery is followed by safe, bounded fetching and evidence storage. Official and primary sources rank first. Retrieved content is untrusted data. Each factual planning statement links to evidence with source, retrieval time, effective date, and freshness. Deterministic policy filters prohibit individual-security recommendations.
